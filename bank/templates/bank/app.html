          Support

          Answers to common questions, or just ask the assistant.

          How do I send money by voice?

          Tap the microphone and say something like “Send 300 to Aman”. EchoMint reads it back. Say “yes” to send. From ₹500 you type your PIN on screen instead.

          Why does it ask me to read numbers?

          Reading fresh, random digits proves it's really you speaking right now. A recording of your voice can't pass, because it won't have those digits.

          The voice check keeps failing

          Speak a little closer to the microphone in a quiet room. If it still fails, choose “Use my PIN instead”. You can also re-record your voice in Profile.

          I entered the wrong PIN three times

          PIN payments are locked for 15 minutes to keep your money safe. Small voice payments still work.

          Someone is pressuring me to send money

          Stop and hang up. EchoMint will never ask for your PIN out loud. Lower your daily limit in Settings if you're worried.
